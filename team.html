How to communicate with the teaching team

📋

Always use Ed first — including for private matters

All unit questions should go through Ed, whether public or private. If your question is personal, sensitive, about special consideration, or about personal circumstances, use a private post on Ed so only the teaching team can see it.

Please do not email staff directly first. Ed keeps everything in one place and ensures the right person sees your message. If your private Ed post has not been answered after three working days, you may then email the Chief Examiner directly and include FIT2109 in the subject line.

⏱️

Response time

We aim to respond to all Ed posts within two working days. If your question is time-sensitive (e.g. close to an assessment deadline), post as early as possible — last-minute questions may not receive a response in time.

🤝

Be respectful to everyone

This unit brings together students from many different backgrounds and experience levels. Please be polite and considerate in all your interactions — with your classmates, your teaching assistants, and your lecturers. There are no stupid questions, and everyone is here to learn. A respectful environment makes it easier for everyone to ask for help and to share what they know.
